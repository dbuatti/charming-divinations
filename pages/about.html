About

Teaching that leaves room for you

Linda Webster is a reader and a teacher of elemental ritual. What unites it is a belief that people already know more than they have been told.

How she teaches

Linda's teaching is intuitive and largely improvised. She prepares the frame and the handouts, then lets the session breathe — spirit guides the content, and it changes from week to week. Her classes have always run for ninety minutes, and in person she has taught two sessions in a day.

What she is careful about is the layer of intermediaries. Linda is direct about the misinformation that circulates in spiritual and witchcraft circles: people should be able to connect directly with the divine rather than routing that through priests, gurus or a prescribed lineage. And she is equally direct that a teaching which sits badly with you is probably not yours to take on.

A taste, not a doctrine

When it comes to readings, Linda's stated aim is to genuinely help people, not to perform the role of a predictive figure. She describes her preference as giving people a taste of what is, and then letting them choose their own path. That philosophy runs through the whole practice — the courses, the ritual circles, and the one-to-one work.

The five elements

The elemental framework is the spine of the practice. Water as hydration, movement and change; air as breath and clarity; fire as transformation; earth as grounded knowing; spirit as integration. The Council of the Root is where those threads are pulled together into a single live session, and the beginner's course is built on the same five.

Her own account of how to hold it is worth repeating: participants receive what their higher selves intend, and anything that feels uncomfortable is not something to take on.

What she is building

The Basically Brilliant Beginner is Linda's own course — a beginner's path through the elemental foundations, from colour and chakras through protection, scrying, tarot and crystal work, to meeting your own guides. The first module, Water, has been taught. The rest is in development.

The Council

What a session is

  • ✦An opening blessing, a crystal bowl, and breath to let go of what was carried in.
  • ✦Teaching on the element — what it governs, and the myth that holds it.
  • ✦A ritual simple enough to do at a kitchen table.
  • ✦A guided meditation, then the circle for what surfaced.

Her approach

  • ✦Ninety minutes, two sessions a day when in person.
  • ✦Handouts carry the structure; the teaching stays responsive.
  • ✦Spirit guides the content, and it changes week to week.
  • ✦You choose your own path — that is the whole point.

The through-line

Water, and what came after

The first Council session, in three movements.

The teaching

Change you don't fight

Water as the foundational element — movement, change, and learning to endure hardship without looking at it as lack. The Inuit goddess Sedna as the warning against swimming against the current.

The ritual

Two glasses

A simple working for change: all the water in the left glass, then a little poured each day through the lunar cycle, affirming the change you want.

The meditation

Down the stairs

White light forming a protective shell, then down withered wooden stairs to a private sand-dune beach — and the discovery that you can breathe underwater.
