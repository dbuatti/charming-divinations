The shape of it

What a session actually looks like

A loose frame, so the teaching can move with what the room needs.

01 · Arrive

Settle in. Sometimes a short grounding practice, sometimes just a shared breath. Ninety minutes, two sessions a day if in person.

02 · Teach

The core of the session, guided rather than scripted. Handouts carry the structure so the teaching itself can stay responsive.

03 · Practise

Hands-on work — a ritual, a breath technique, a bodywork method. Enough structure to be safe, enough space to find your own way through it.

04 · Share

Questions, and what people noticed. The part that usually matters most, and the part Linda never scripts.
