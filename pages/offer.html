What I Offer

Readings, casting, and circles

Everything here is offered as a conversation rather than a verdict. Whatever you arrive with, the aim is for you to leave clearer than you came.

Clarity · Guidance · Empowerment

Readings

A reading is not a forecast. It is a way of looking at the question you are actually carrying — and being helped to answer it yourself.

A tarot reading

One to one · 60 minutes · online or in person

The cards laid out and read for the situation you are in. Linda's stated aim is to genuinely help you make your own decisions — not to act like a predictive figure. You are welcome to take what resonates and leave what does not.

— fee to be confirmed

A reading in other forms

One to one · 60 minutes

Not every question is best asked of a deck. Other forms of divination include scrying, the pendulum, runes, and oracle cards — chosen with you for what you are actually asking.

— fee to be confirmed

Intention · Energy · Possibility

Charm casting

A working built around a question you are really asking. Charms are chosen for their associations and combined into a set, then the working is closed and the charms kept — for you, for as long as they hold meaning.

This sits within the wider practice of elemental work: the same five elements that structure the Council of the Root and the beginner's course.

Courses

The Basically Brilliant Beginner

Linda's beginner's course in the elemental foundations — five modules, each closing with a live session. For anyone starting from the beginning, which is most people.

II · Air
