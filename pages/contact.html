Before you write

Common questions

What if I don't know what I need?

That is the most common place to start. Describe what's happening and we'll work out the shape of the session together.

Are sessions recorded?

Council circles are recorded, so you can revisit the guided meditation. One-to-one sessions are not, unless you ask for that.

What if a teaching doesn't fit me?

Then it isn't yours to take on. Linda's own position is that you should leave behind anything that feels uncomfortable — and that choosing your own path is the whole point.
