The grounded half

Mystical insight, grounded guidance

Before the ritual and the reading, there is the training: kinesiology through the College of Complimentary Medicine — anatomy and pathology, client intake assessment, postural balance and correction, and clinic hours with real people and real complaints.

It is the same discipline. Attend properly, read what is actually there, and act from that.
